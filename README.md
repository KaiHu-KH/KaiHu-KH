## Hi there 👋, I'm Kai Hu (胡凯)

I am currently a Researcher at Nex-AGI, working on LLM Agent research, with broader interests in MLLMs, LLM Agent, Agent Memory, Agent RL, and Multi-Agent Systems. Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist of **Multimodal Intelligence Team in Microsoft CoreAI** <img src='./images/microsoft_logo.svg' style='width: 4em;'>, where I worked on OCR, Document Intelligence, RAG and MLLMs research, and led the development of the industry-leading [Azure Layout API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/document-intelligence/concept-layout). 

I obtained my Ph.D. degree from the joint Ph.D. program between [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://ustc.edu.cn/) and [Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/) in 2024, under the supervision of [Prof. Qiang Huo](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/qianghuo/) and [Prof. Jun Du](http://staff.ustc.edu.cn/~jundu/). During my Ph.D., I interned at DeepSeek, contributing to DeepSeek OCR, DeepSeek VL2, DeepSeek V3, and DeepSeek R1, and at MSRA, working on [Microsoft OneOCR](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/computer-vision/overview-ocr) and [Document Intelligence](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/ai-services/ai-document-intelligence). I have published 10+ papers <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Gt3I5lgAAAAJ'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?logo=Google%20Scholar&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.jsdelivr.net%2Fgh%2FKaiHu-KH%2FKaiHu-KH.github.io@google-scholar-stats%2Fgs_data_shieldsio.json&labelColor=f6f6f6&color=9cf&style=flat&label=citations"></a> at top international AI journals and conferences, and one of my papers received the **Best Paper Award** at ICDAR 2021.
